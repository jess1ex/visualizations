Schwarz–Christoffel transformation

Choose a point on either graph to see its match.

Input z-plane

Output w-plane

Changing a finite angle also changes the angle at ∞.

The equation

f(z)=C1 ∫z0z ∏k=1n−1 (ζ−xk)αk−1 dζ+C2
z, ζ
z is the input point and ζ is the integration variable.
xk, n
Each finite prevertex xk lies on the real axis. The nth prevertex is at ∞.
αk
The interior angle at corner k is αkπ. The angle at ∞ follows from the polygon’s angle sum.
C1
A complex multiplier. Its magnitude scales the polygon and its argument rotates it. Enter the argument in units of π.
z0, C2
The integral starts at z0. Its image is C2, which translates the polygon.

How to solve a Schwarz–Christoffel problem

  1. Label the polygon vertices counterclockwise, starting wherever is convenient.
  2. Assign the corresponding prevertices in increasing real-axis order. In this visualization, the last prevertex is at ∞.
  3. Write the derivative using the interior angles. An angle of αkπ gives an exponent of αk − 1 at each finite prevertex.
  4. Choose C1 so one edge points in the correct direction and has the correct length.
  5. Choose the additive constant C2 so one vertex lands at its required coordinate.
