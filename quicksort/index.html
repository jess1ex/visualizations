Quicksort

Partition the array around a pivot, then recursively sort the ranges on either side.

Enter 1–12 comma-separated integers from −999 to 999.

Array

    Blue marks the active range, purple outlines the pivot, orange marks compared or swapped positions, and green marks values in their final positions.

    Recursive calls

    Each call shows its inclusive, zero-based bounds. Calls appear when entered and remain visible after returning.

      Partition rule

      Use the rightmost value as pivot. Scan left to right, swapping values less than or equal to the pivot into the left region. Then move the pivot between the two regions. The regions may still be unsorted until their recursive calls return.
